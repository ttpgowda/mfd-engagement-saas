# Understanding Mutual Funds

A **mutual fund** is a financial vehicle that pools money from many investors to purchase securities like stocks, bonds, money market instruments, and other assets.

## Why Invest in Mutual Funds?

- **Professional Management**: Fund managers research and select securities for you.
- **Diversification**: You can own a small slice of many companies.
- **Affordability**: You can start with as little as ₹500 via SIP.

> "Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. He who understands it, earns it... he who doesn't... pays it." - Albert Einstein

## Types of Mutual Funds

1. **Equity Funds**: Invest in stocks. High risk, high return.
2. **Debt Funds**: Invest in bonds. Lower risk, stable returns.
3. **Hybrid Funds**: Mix of both.
