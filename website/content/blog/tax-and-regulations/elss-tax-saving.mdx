# Why ELSS is the Best Tax Saver

Under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, you can claim a deduction of up to ₹1.5 Lakhs. While PPF and FD are popular, **ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme)** offers the shortest lock-in period and highest potential returns.

## ELSS vs PPF vs FD

| Feature | ELSS | PPF | Tax-Saving FD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lock-in** | 3 Years | 15 Years | 5 Years |
| **Returns** | Market Linked (12-15%*) | Fixed (~7.1%) | Fixed (~6-7%) |
| **Tax on Returns** | 10% on gains > ₹1L | Tax Free | Taxable |

*> Returns are historical and not guaranteed.*

## Strategy

Don't wait until March! Start a monthly SIP in an ELSS fund today to spread your tax burden throughout the year.
